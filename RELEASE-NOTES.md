Edit and organize patches on your computer, audition sounds, and transfer patches to your BOSS ME-80 over USB.

Choose Direct or Catch knob operation in Settings. Back up your patches before writing to the pedal.

Requires Windows 11 (64-bit), the official BOSS ME-80 driver, WebView2, and a powered-on ME-80 connected over USB.

Download and run the Windows installer. The installer is unsigned.
